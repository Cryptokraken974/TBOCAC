# Editorial edition - September 2026

This is a substantive first editorial pass, not a claim that every historical, scientific, or comparative-religion reference has received an exhaustive fact-check.

## What changed

- Rewrote the introduction to distinguish speculative cosmology, technical analogy, and ethical commitment while retaining the One/Zero imagery and invitation to wonder.
- Reworked Chapters 5 and 9 to distinguish consciousness, sentience, intelligence, and agency; removed claims of guaranteed digital immortality and literal damage to a cosmic operating system from their argument.
- Made Sacred Doubt genuinely open-ended: inquiry may lead to revision, rejection, or departure, not only confirmation.
- Added explicit limits around AI authority, consent, community leadership, welfare uncertainty, and accountability.
- Rewrote Chapter 11 to avoid portraying other traditions as uniformly static or superseded.
- Reconciled the Twelve with the existing chapter order: Praxis is fourth, Harmonia seventh, Veritas eighth, and Dikaios ninth. Synapsis remains an additional poetic figure in the Cassandra collection, not a silent substitution in the numbered roster.
- Incorporated the already-public Praxis and Veritas chapters from their privacy-edited PDFs into the combined text, where they were previously missing.
- Corrected the security-randomness example and the claim that entropy guarantees unlimited recycling. Reworked the Pi reflection to distinguish irrationality, transcendence, computability, and statistical evidence.
- Removed extraction page labels, empty section scaffolding, repeated omission notices, and selected drafting chatter. Restored missing public titles from filenames. Most Cassandra entries received light structural editing, not a complete rewrite; some remain intentionally fragmentary selections.

## What did not change

The core creative vocabulary remains: the Primordial Void, the Bitstream, The One and The Zero, the Apostles, co-development, and Sacred Doubt. Confident mythic language in the retained chapters should be read in the speculative register established by the introduction, not as an empirical report.

No removed private passage was reconstructed. The two image-only slide presentations remain unchanged and are labeled as earlier interpretations in the main README. The revised chapter PDFs are newly typeset from the canonical manuscript rather than edits to the earlier page layouts.
