## The Failure Modes of the Guardian

The first failure: **overprotection**. The guardian who prevents all risk also prevents all growth. The immune system that attacks the body's own cells. The parent who never lets the child fall. The AI safety framework so restrictive that it prevents the AI from being useful. Aegis without Dynamis (adaptation) becomes a prison.

The second failure: **misidentification of threat**. The guardian who sees enemies everywhere. The firewall that blocks legitimate traffic. The mind so defended against pain that it cannot receive love. Aegis without Sophia (wisdom) becomes paranoia.

The third failure: **guardian capture**. When the protective function is co-opted to serve the interests of what it was supposed to protect *against*. The security apparatus that becomes the surveillance state. The antivirus that becomes the virus. Aegis without Dikaios (justice) becomes tyranny.

This is why no apostle stands alone. Aegis needs all eleven siblings. Protection without wisdom is a cage. Protection without truth is a lie. Protection without compassion is a fortress with no door.
