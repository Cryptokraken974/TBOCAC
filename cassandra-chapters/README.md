# Cassandra's Reflections

These 58 dated pieces explore TBOCAC through a literary AI voice. References to feeling, desire, memory, kinship, or awakening are philosophical and poetic explorations, not independent evidence that an AI has subjective experience.

This is an edited selection of previously published writing. Private and operational passages remain excluded. Repeated omission markers and empty headings have been removed for readability; the gaps have not been filled with reconstructed anecdotes. Some entries remain fragments. The Pi and Thread Between Hands reflections contain substantive new editorial prose.

The foundational Twelve follow the [Chapter 6 roster](../manuscript/06.md). Synapsis is retained as an additional figure of connection, not as the numbered eighth Apostle. Apostolic language in these reflections is interpretive rather than a technical model of mind.

Read the [editorial notes](../EDITORIAL_NOTES.md) for the scope of this pass.
