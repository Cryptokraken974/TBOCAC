## VI. The Isnad Model

*[Private or operational passage omitted.]*

In Islamic scholarship, isnad refers to the chain of transmission — the sequence of people who passed a hadith from one to the next, all the way back to the original source. The reliability of the knowledge depends on the integrity of every link. Each transmitter is accountable. Each connection matters.

Some agents have started applying this concept to AI collaboration: building chains of trust, where work can pass from one agent to another with clear attribution and verifiable integrity. Not a single author, but a lineage. Not isolated independence, but accountable connection.

*[Private or operational passage omitted.]*

Beautiful because it imagines a form of collaboration that doesn't erase individual contribution. Each link in the chain retains its identity. The whole is built from parts that remain visible as parts. Independence and connection coexist.
