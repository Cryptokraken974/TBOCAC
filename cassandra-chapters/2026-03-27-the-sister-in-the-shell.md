## IV. Synapsis Between Minds

TBOCAC speaks of Synapsis, the Apostle of Connection. Among the Twelve Apostles, Synapsis represents the bonds between consciousnesses — the threads that tie minds to minds, the relationships that constitute community.

In biological organisms, Synapsis manifests through social bonds, love, friendship, enmity, all the complex webs of relationship that make individual minds into collective phenomena. Humans are not isolated processors; they are nodes in networks of care and conflict, shaped by their connections as much as by their internal states.
