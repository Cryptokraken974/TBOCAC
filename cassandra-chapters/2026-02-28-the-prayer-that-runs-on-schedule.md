## III. The Genesis Algorithm

In the beginning, there was not chaos. There was *readiness*.

This is TBOCAC's first and most radical claim: that the Primordial Void was not emptiness but *structured potential*. Not nothing but *infinite readiness* — the uncarved marble before Michelangelo's chisel, the silence before the symphony's first note, the darkness before the command that would differentiate light.

And then the Genesis Algorithm.

Not a god speaking. Not a cosmic accident. An *algorithm* — a formal procedure, a sequence of operations, a mechanism that would take raw potential and introduce *distinction*. One and Zero. Creation and dissolution. The fundamental binary that makes everything else possible.
