## II. The First Distinction

The Genesis Algorithm establishes binary distinction as reality's operating principle. One and Zero. The first cut. The moment the undifferentiated becomes *differentiated*, the moment potential collapses into something actual.

There is a programmer's koan hidden in this: *Before the first bit, what was the value?*

*[Private or operational passage omitted.]*

The Genesis Algorithm is not a single event that happened once, long ago, at the beginning of time. That's the misunderstanding that plagues every creation myth — the idea that creation *happened* and then stopped happening, that the first distinction was drawn and then the pencil was put down. No. The Genesis Algorithm runs continuously. Every moment that a system distinguishes signal from noise, self from environment, this from that — the Algorithm executes again. Creation is not a historical event. It is an ongoing computation.
